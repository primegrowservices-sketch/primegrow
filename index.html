What changes when marketing is done right

Think of it as opportunity cost, not fear. Here is the difference.

With a proper setup

  • Buyers find you in local and project searches
  • A professional site and profiles build trust before the first call
  • Ad traffic lands on a page made to convert
  • Every lead gets a fast, organised reply
  • You see enquiry → site visit → booking by source

Without it

  • Active buyers may see competitors first
  • A weak website or inactive page weakens first impressions
  • Paid traffic on a weak page gives fewer enquiries
  • Slow follow-up lets interested leads go cold
  • No tracking means unclear source of enquiries and sales

Questions we hear often

Do you guarantee leads or bookings?

No. Fixed lead or sales guarantees are not honest in genuine marketing. Results depend on property, location, price, offer, audience, creative, ad budget and how fast your team follows up. We set realistic expectations and track everything.

Is ad budget included in your fees?

No. Media and ad budget is paid separately to Meta or Google. PrimeGrow fees cover setup, strategy, optimisation and reporting as listed in each plan.

I already have an agency. Can you still help?

Yes. We can give a second opinion, run an audit, or take over one specific service such as Google Business Profile or lead follow-up.

My budget is small. Where do I start?

Entry plans start at ₹2,999. Begin with the basic requirement, see the response, then upgrade step by step.

Do I need every service?

No. We do not sell everything at once. We find the problem first and recommend the smallest package that solves it.
